What can't be added

Sites that only show jobs in a browser, pages behind a login or a bot check, search pages robots.txt blocks, and sites whose terms forbid bots can't be added. Copying a search page and picking parts of it with CSS selectors isn't offered: it breaks easily and is often not allowed. For boards that forbid bots, reading their alert emails is the allowed route, as a later feature.
